SIH EVALUATION &SHORTLISTING WEB APPLICATION
Functional Requirements & Workflow Specification
Prepared for: VIT - Final College SIH Evaluation
Purpose: Digital evaluation, shortlisting, jury scoring, tallying and final result generation
Technical Support: 2R under the institutional MoU
Document Version: 1.0
Evaluation Model: Two-round evaluation with configurable Round 1 shortlisting and 45 + 5 final outcome after Round 2
Core Selection Rule
Round 1 does not produce the final 45 + 5. Round 1 only produces a configurable Top N shortlist. Round 2 is conducted for those shortlisted teams, where every jury member marked Present can score every shortlisted team. The final ranking after Round 2 produces 45 Selected teams and 5 Standby teams.
Designed as a configurable college evaluation system rather than a hard-coded one-time form.
1. Purpose and Objective
The proposed web application will provide VIT with a centralized platform to manage the complete internal SIH evaluation process, from team registration and evaluator assignment through Round 1 shortlisting, Round 2 jury scoring, final ranking, comments, tallying and report generation.
The system is intended to reduce manual Excel consolidation, avoid score-entry errors, support different evaluator combinations for different teams, maintain a complete audit trail, and make final shortlisting transparent and reproducible.
1.1 Key Objectives
Maintain a single master database of SIH teams, SIH IDs, project details, group members and evaluation status.
Allow the college to assign internal evaluators, external evaluators, or both to each team during Round 1.
Allow evaluation criteria and marks columns to be configured by the administrator instead of being hard-coded.
Automatically tally marks and comments and generate a configurable Top N shortlist after Round 1.
Conduct Round 2 using a common jury pool where every jury member who is present can score every shortlisted team.
Generate the final merit list after Round 2 with 45 Selected teams and the next 5 teams as Standby.
Provide downloadable, auditable reports for administrators, coordinators and final record-keeping.

5.3 Round 1 Score Tallying
Each evaluator submits criterion-wise marks plus comments for the assigned team.
The system calculates each evaluator's total score and normalized percentage where different maximum marks are used.
By default, the team Round 1 score is the average of all valid evaluator submissions for that team. This prevents a team with more evaluators from receiving an unfair numerical advantage.
Internal/external weighting may be made configurable if VIT later chooses to use different evaluator-type weightages; equal weighting should be the default.
A missing evaluator submission must remain Pending and must not silently become a zero score.
The admin dashboard must show evaluation completion so pending teams can be identified before Round 1 is closed.
5.4 Round 1 Shortlisting
Configurable Top N
After Round 1 is completed and locked, the administrator enters or confirms the required shortlist count N. The system ranks eligible teams by the approved Round 1 tally and generates the Top N shortlist for Round 2. N may be changed for different events.
Shortlisted teams automatically move to Round 2.
Round 1 marks remain available in reports and audit records.
By default, Round 1 marks are used for shortlisting only and are not automatically added to the Round 2 final score.
Any carry-forward weightage between rounds should be optional and explicitly enabled by the college if ever required.
6. Round 2 - Final Jury Evaluation
Round 2 is the final evaluation stage for the Top N teams shortlisted from Round 1. Unlike Round 1, teams are not individually mapped to specific evaluators.
6.1 Present-Jury Voting Model
Round 2 Rule
All jury members who are physically/officially present for the Round 2 session and are marked Present in the system will be eligible to enter marks for every shortlisted team.
The admin maintains the Round 2 jury list and marks attendance before or during the final session.
Only jury members with attendance status Present receive Round 2 scoring access.
Every Present jury member sees the same list of shortlisted teams and the same Round 2 evaluation criteria.
Each Present jury member can score every shortlisted team.
A jury member's incomplete scorecard remains Pending until submitted or formally excluded by the authorized administrator.
Jurors should not see other jurors' individual marks before submitting their own scores, reducing scoring influence/bias.
6.2 Round 2 Criteria
Round 2 may use a different set of 3-4 customizable criteria from Round 1. The college can define criterion names, maximum marks and weightages before opening the round.
Sample Criterion
Illustrative Purpose
Technical Depth / Feasibility
Quality and implementability of the technical approach.
Innovation / Uniqueness
Novelty and differentiation of the proposed solution.
Prototype / Demonstration
Working implementation, proof of concept and demonstration quality.
Impact / Presentation
Problem impact, clarity, scalability and jury communication.
The above names are examples only. Final criterion names and marks are to be configured by VIT administrators.
6.3 Round 2 Tallying and Final Ranking
For each shortlisted team, the system totals each jury member's submitted criterion marks.
The final Round 2 team score is calculated using the average of valid submitted scores from all eligible Present juries, unless VIT configures another approved formula.
Round 2 score is the default basis for the final ranking. Round 1 remains a shortlisting-stage score unless carry-forward is explicitly enabled.
The system ranks all Round 2 teams from highest to lowest score after the round is locked.
Final Outcome after Round 2
Rank 1-45: SELECTED for final college SIH nomination/forward process. Rank 46-50: STANDBY. Remaining shortlisted teams: NOT SELECTED. The 45 + 5 classification is generated only after completion of Round 2.
7. Tie and Exceptional-Case Handling
Because final ranks can contain equal scores, the application should make tie handling explicit instead of silently reordering teams.
Scenario
Recommended System Behaviour
Equal total score
Display the teams as Tied and apply the configured tie-break rule only if the college has defined one.
Tie around Rank 45 / 50
Flag prominently for jury/coordinator review before publishing the final selected/standby list.
Evaluator absent after assignment
Admin may reassign/remove the evaluator with an audit log before locking Round 1.
Round 2 jury marked Present but unable to complete
Submission remains Pending; authorized admin may formally exclude it with reason before final lock.
Team absent / withdrawn
Admin marks status accordingly; team is excluded from ranking based on configured policy.
Score correction after submission
Only authorized reopen/edit action; old value, new value, user and timestamp retained in audit history.

11. Controls, Security and Auditability
Role-based authentication and access control.
Each evaluation submission records evaluator/jury ID, timestamp and round.
Auto-save or Save Draft to protect marks during long evaluation sessions.
Final Submit confirmation before locking an evaluator scorecard.
Admin reopening of submitted marks only with a mandatory reason and audit record.
Juror scores hidden from other jurors until the evaluation is complete/published.
Round-level lock to prevent accidental changes after shortlisting/finalization.
Database backup/export before final result publishing.
HTTPS and institution-approved deployment/security practices for production use.

14. Acceptance Criteria
Admin can create at least one complete evaluation event without developer intervention.
A team can be evaluated in Round 1 by internal only, external only, both, or multiple evaluators.
Round 1 criteria and maximum marks can be changed from the admin panel.
Top N can be set/changed by the admin and the correct number of eligible teams moves to Round 2.
Round 2 juries can be marked Present/Absent and only Present juries receive scoring access.
Every Present Round 2 jury can score every shortlisted team.
System produces a final rank based on the configured Round 2 tally.
System correctly shows 45 Selected + 5 Standby after Round 2, not after Round 1.
Comments, score details and evaluation completion can be exported for records.
Submitted scores are locked and any authorized corrections are auditable.

Recommended Governance Principle
The software should calculate and present results according to the rules configured by VIT, but it should not independently make discretionary academic decisions. Final publication should require an authorized VIT coordinator action.
